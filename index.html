A few things to know.

Are the packs free?

The original starter demo is free. Football and anime collections have not been released yet. Future packs will show their price and license clearly.

Can I use the demo in my videos?

Yes. The Vortex Starter mascot was created for this site. You can use it in personal and monetized video edits. This permission does not cover third-party music, characters, logos or footage you add.

Will there be video-format packs?

That is the plan. Currently, only the animated SVG demo is available. No MP4, WebM or project-file packs have been uploaded yet.

Where can I send suggestions?

Visit V0rtexEd1t on YouTube and leave a comment on a video. Suggest a theme or character for a future drop.
